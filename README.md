## What it is

A single-page photobooth that lives entirely in your browser. Point the webcam at yourself, hit the shutter, and download a printable photo strip in seconds. No accounts, no servers, no uploads — every photo stays on your device.

## Features

* **Webcam capture** with countdown, shutter flash, and shutter sound.
* **Shot counts** of 2, 4, or 6 photos per strip.
* **Strip formats**: vertical strip, horizontal strip, square, and Polaroid cards.
* **Aspect ratios**: 1:1, 4:3, 3:4, 16:9, and 9:16 — pick a shape and every cell follows.
* **Themes** to recolor paper, ink, and accents.
* **Share or download** the finished strip directly from your browser.
* **Front/back camera switching** on devices with multiple cameras.
* **Privacy-first**: nothing leaves your device.
